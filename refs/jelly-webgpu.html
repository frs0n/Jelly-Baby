Inside the experiment

A tetrahedral, nearly incompressible neo-Hookean solid, integrated with fixed-step XPBD. Gravity, mass, inertia, local grabbing, elastic recoil, internal damping and frictional floor contact are simulated in SI units.

Wet-surface transmission, Fresnel reflections and absorption accompany geometry-traced, RGB refractive caustics. These are real-time approximations—not a perfectly accurate laboratory model. No tearing or self-collision; extreme folding is limited. View transmission is screen-space, and caustics use a finite photon budget and a planar receiver.

Drag the jelly to pull and throw. Drag the background to orbit; scroll to zoom. N: nudge · R: reset · Space: pause. Requires WebGPU, a secure context (HTTPS or localhost), and internet access for pinned Three.js modules.
